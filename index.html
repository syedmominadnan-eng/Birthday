🎉 Happy Birthday Ma'am Asma 🎂

Today is a very special day because it is the birthday of a person who is not only my teacher, but also someone who supported me, understood me, and stood by me when I needed it the most.

Ma’am, some teachers only teach from books, but some teachers leave memories that stay with us forever. You are one of those special people for me.

I still remember the moments when you supported me like a little brother, not just like a student. The way you understood me, helped me, and sometimes even joked with me made our bond different and special.

I can never forget the small memories we created together. Like the time when my phone situation happened in school and you supported me. The way you handled that moment with kindness showed me that you really cared and understood me.

I also remember the fun moments, our conversations, the laughter, and the memories we created. The day we enjoyed pizza, spent time together, talked, laughed, and even when my shoe got damaged and we found a funny way to manage it — these simple moments became unforgettable memories.

Maybe these moments look small to others, but for me they are special because they remind me of your kindness, support, and the care you showed while still being my respected teacher.

Ma’am, life keeps changing and sometimes paths become different. Maybe we will meet again many times, or maybe life will take us to different places. But I believe the respect, prayers, and beautiful memories will always remain.

Thank you for supporting me when I needed someone, for understanding me, and for being such an amazing teacher.

I pray that Allah blesses you with endless happiness, success, good health, and a life full of beautiful moments.

🎂 Happy Birthday once again, Ma’am Asma! ❤️

May Allah always keep a smile on your face.

— Momin
